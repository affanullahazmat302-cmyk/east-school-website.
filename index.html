About Us

A Legacy of Quality Education in District Nowshera

The East School & College, located on Swabi Road in Jehangira, District Nowshera, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, is one of the area's most respected private institutions. The school aims to produce highly educated and peace-loving citizens in the light of the ideology of Islam and Pakistan, and to sustain and develop quality education for the children of Jehangira and surrounding areas.

From early years through matriculation and college-level studies, The East combines strong academic foundations with character building, discipline and modern teaching methodologies — preparing students for board examinations of BISE Mardan and beyond.

Admissions

How to Join The East

Admission is based on merit. Except Play Group and Nursery, all admissions require an entry test, subject to the availability of seats in the respective class.

Registration

Register before the closing date at the school office, Swabi Road Jehangira, to appear in the entry test.

Entry Test

Held every year in the 1st week of April, based on the courses of the previous class. Result is displayed the next day.

Merit List

Minimum 50% marks required in the primary section entry test; 70% for middle and high sections.

Enrollment

Complete admission formalities and fee deposit to secure the seat. Dues are payable by the 10th of each month.

📝

  • English

Fee Concessions

  • One seat reserved in every class for poor students and orphans — merit criteria apply where candidates compete.
  • Half-fee concession for families with more than two siblings (third and junior-most child).
  • The Principal may allow concession for education-department employees at his discretion.
  • No concession is admissible for absence periods.
📋

Fee Rules

  • All dues must be deposited by the 10th of each month.
  • Dues must be cleared before examinations begin, otherwise the student cannot sit the exam.
  • Summer-vacation fees are deposited in advance.
  • Late fee fine: Rs. 10 per day.
  • Names of students with 3 months of unpaid dues are struck off the school roll.
